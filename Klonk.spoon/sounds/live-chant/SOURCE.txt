Set 'live-chant' is performed, not sampled per key: Klonk forwards your typing to
performer/gregorian.html, a live Web Audio page.

Each keystroke sings the next syllable of real Gregorian chant (Dies irae, Kyrie XI,
Veni Creator, Requiem aeternam, Viderunt omnes, in turn); typing speed sets the tempo;
holding keys sustains the voice; a comma breathes; Backspace re-sings the previous
syllable; a period or Return sings the phrase's cadence onto the final with an organum
fifth beneath, and the next phrase begins with your next keystroke.

Voices and repertoire come from Synth_Gregorian (Brendan James Lynskey, MIT); the voices
are VocalSet recordings (CC BY 4.0). See performer/CREDITS.md.
