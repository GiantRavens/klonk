Recorded Telegraph set curated from:

- retro-terminals assets/sounds/sfx/telegraph.m4a
- retro-terminals assets/sounds/sfx/telegraph2.m4a
- retro-terminals assets/sounds/sfx/telegraph3.m4a

The first two recordings provide short armature strikes for typing and mouse
events. The third provides the longer physical actions reserved for Space and
Return. Sources were decoded to mono 44.1 kHz, transient-trimmed, faded, and
peak-normalized with tools/make_set.py. No synthetic bell is mixed into Return.
