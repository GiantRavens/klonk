Set 'khoomei' is performed, not sampled per key: Klonk forwards your typing to
performer/khoomei.html, Mongolian and Tuvan throat singing on the open steppe.

Each keystroke plays the next real sygyt note (the whistled overtone melody above a
drone), in the order the singer sang them, over a soft drone tuned to his own
fundamental. A galloping hoof rhythm, a morin khuur bowing a fifth drone, and deep
kargyraa recordings join as the sentence grows. Typing speed sets the gallop; a held key
lets the note ring; a comma breathes; Backspace sings the last note again; a period or
Return plays the singer's own ending, the final note of a take.

Recordings: throat singing by Alfonso Perez, recorded at MTG (Universitat Pompeu Fabra),
uploaded to Freesound by Bram: packs "Alfonso - Sygyt" (freesound.org/people/Bram/packs/1190)
and "Alfonso - Kargyraa" (freesound.org/people/Bram/packs/1189). Licensed CC BY 4.0
(creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0). Sliced into notes by tools/build_khoomei.py from
the public HQ previews, pinned by sound ID. Morin khuur, hooves and the drone bed are
synthesized; without the recordings the performer synthesizes the sygyt whistle itself.
