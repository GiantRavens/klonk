Set 'techno-build' is performed, not sampled per key: Klonk forwards your typing to
performer/techno.html, a live groovebox synthesized in an AudioWorklet. No samples.

A sentence is a build and Return is the drop. The first keystroke starts the kick;
more keystrokes add off-beat hats, claps and sixteenths, a rolling bass, then a snare
roll and noise riser. Typing speed sets the tempo (118-140 BPM, eased in at each bar);
holding keys opens the bass filter; a comma drops the kick for the next bar; Backspace
scratches; a period or Return lands POW on a fresh downbeat, then two bars of the full
groove. Stop typing and the layers fall away a bar at a time; four quiet bars end it.
